Email Delivery

To enable real email delivery on your internal server, set ENGCMMS_MAIL_SERVER (and optionally ENGCMMS_MAIL_PORT, ENGCMMS_MAIL_USE_TLS, ENGCMMS_MAIL_USERNAME, ENGCMMS_MAIL_PASSWORD, ENGCMMS_MAIL_SENDER).

Until then the system runs in preview mode: all generated emails are fully rendered and recorded in the email log, but nothing is actually sent — ideal for local office testing.
